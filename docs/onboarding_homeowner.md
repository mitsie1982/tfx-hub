# Onboarding Guide: Homeowners

Welcome! This guide will help you post jobs and connect with professionals.

## Steps

1. Register and verify your contact details.
2. Post a job request (describe your needs).
3. Review professional responses and select a provider.
4. Rate your experience after job completion.
